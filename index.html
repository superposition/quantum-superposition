Definitions and scope
Concepts Distinction Related models
Amplitude / probability An amplitude is complex. A probability is real and nonnegative. For a normalized state and a chosen outcome k, P(k) = |⟨k|ψ⟩|². InterferenceComplex amplitude
Superposition / mixture A coherent superposition retains relative phase. A diagonal mixture has no off-diagonal coherence in the selected basis. Equal populations alone do not determine a state. Pure statesDephasing
State space / physical space The Bloch ball represents single-qubit density matrices. Positions in this ball encode measurement statistics, not locations of a particle. Probability sections
Dephasing / recorded measurement Dephasing reduces ensemble coherence without selecting an outcome. An ideal projective measurement conditioned on a recorded outcome updates one copy to the corresponding eigenstate. DephasingMeasurement
One copy / ensemble One shot records one outcome. Batch frequencies estimate Born probabilities from independent preparations. Repeated measurement of the same collapsed copy in the same basis is deterministic. Measurement
Single qubit / entanglement A single Bloch vector does not describe correlations between multiple systems. The models in this gallery do not simulate entanglement. Gallery-wide limit

State convention

|ψ⟩ = √(1 − p)|0⟩ + exp(iφ)√p |1⟩

p = P(1) in the Z basis. Amplitudes are normalized. Global phase does not affect probabilities.

Rendering and computation

vgpu renders the field, phase map, and state-space geometry. The graphics are classical computations. Spatial-field coordinates are dimensionless; no full Schrödinger evolution is solved.
